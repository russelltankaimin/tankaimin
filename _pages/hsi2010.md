## Lecture 1  
### Celestial Mechanics  
- Fields of Physics which study the motions of objects in outer space  

### The *GEOCENTRIC* Model  
- Old way of studying motions of celestial objects  
- Earth is the center of the universe  
- Supported by 2 observations  
    - Earth seems not to move from someone standing on Earth, **FEELS** solid, stable  
    - Sun, Moon and planets **APPEARS** to revolve around Earth once per day  
- Stars appeared to be fixed on a celestial sphere rotating about once each day about an axis through the geographical poles of Earth  
- Today's astrological sign is say, Leo, because the Sun is arond the region of the Leo constellation (hence, you cannot see Leo in the night sky)  
- Sequence : Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo (West to East)  
- Sun moves eastwards (on a yearly basis) whereas the moon also but on a monthly basis **COMPARED TO THE STARS**. They will be the east of where it is. But they actually move westwards.  
- Stars move westwards instead.  

### Aristotelian Physics  
- Form of natural science/philosophy described in the works of Aristotle.  
- According to Aristotle, the celestial objects are embedded in concentric spheres that rotate at **FIXED RATES**
- Motion is thought to be uniform circular motion  
- Celestial spheres are composed of the special element aether, the use is to actually revolve the object around Earth.  
- Order of spheres from Earth outward is dependent on the period of revolution around the Earth.
